## 6. Misleading Headline Number

A potentially misleading headline number is:

> **100% relevance on the human-reviewed subset**

This number is technically correct for the evaluated sample: all 10 reviewed cases were judged relevant. However, it should **not** be interpreted as evidence that the overall reply-generation system achieves 100% quality.

The sample contains only **10 cases** and was reviewed by **one human rater**. On the same subset, the positive rate was **70% for helpfulness** and only **30% for grounding**.

Therefore, the 100% relevance figure is useful as a limited observation, but is misleading if presented without the sample size and the other evaluation dimensions.
